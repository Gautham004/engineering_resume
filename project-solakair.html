Solakair · Feb – May 2026

Aerospace engineering internship at Solakair, working across hardware integration, electrical subsystem checkout, and interface control documentation to support bring-up and verification of integrated systems.

What I Did

  • Accelerated hardware bring-up and verification by performing pinout verification, continuity checks, and procedural checkout across integrated electrical subsystems — reducing bench test cycle time through structured pre-test discipline.
  • Improved interface handoff across teams by maintaining configuration-controlled CAD and interface updates, documenting as-built vs. as-designed deltas, and supporting interface control documentation for design and manufacturing — reducing repeated integration issues at handoff boundaries.
  • Strengthened requirements verification during integration by executing structured checkout procedures, tracking discrepancies, and applying contingency-oriented troubleshooting for off-nominal bench behavior — enabling faster pre-test readiness across integration milestones.

What I Learned

  • Hardware integration discipline starts before the hardware arrives — clear interface documentation and configuration control prevent the issues that show up as bench test failures.
  • As-built vs. as-designed delta tracking is not overhead, it is the mechanism by which integration teams catch deviations before they become system-level problems.
  • Structured checkout procedures make off-nominal behavior tractable — when something fails, a documented baseline is what lets you isolate the fault rather than guess at it.
