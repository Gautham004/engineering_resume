A communications subsystem concept for a Mars orbital reflective constellation - relay, direct-to-Earth, and inter-satellite link trade studies, with link budgets to back them up.

Overview

PROMETHEUS is a concept Mars orbital constellation. My slice was the comms subsystem - what links you carry, when you relay, how they close, and what that costs in mass, power, and complexity.

Problem

Mars comms is constrained by geometry, range, and limited ground-station availability. A constellation has to make tradeoffs between direct-to-Earth (DTE) bursts, relay through existing Mars-orbit assets, and inter-satellite links (ISL) within the constellation itself.

Approach

  • Built link budgets in MATLAB for DTE, relay, and ISL paths at representative geometries.
  • Set up a trade-study matrix scoring architectures on data return, latency, mass, and power.
  • Captured assumptions explicitly so the trade can be re-run when downstream subsystems update their numbers.
  • Wrote a short design brief that other subsystem leads could read in one sitting.

Outcome

  • A defensible architecture recommendation, with link budgets and assumptions visible in one place.
  • Cleaner shared language across subsystems thanks to the trade matrix.
  • A lot of practice doing systems-engineering work where the right answer depends on what your team values most.
