Overview

A running body of CFD & aero analysis work: airfoil comparisons, design-point sweeps, and validation of lower-fidelity panel-method results against RANS solutions.

Problem

Panel methods are fast but lie at high alpha; RANS is slow but expensive to mis-mesh. The interesting question is: when can you trust the cheap tool, and where do you need to pay for the expensive one?

Approach

  • Generated airfoil polars in XFOIL across a Reynolds sweep relevant to UAV / DBF cruise points.
  • Built ANSYS Fluent meshes with a documented y+ target; ran a mesh-convergence study before any production runs.
  • Compared XFLR5 / XFOIL CL/CD/CM to RANS at matched conditions; flagged where panel-method assumptions broke down.
  • Wrote MATLAB post-processing scripts so polar comparisons are reproducible across cases.

Outcome

  • Trustable airfoil polars - with uncertainty noted - that downstream design work can lean on.
  • A repeatable mesh-convergence template for follow-on CFD work.
  • Sharper intuition for when panel methods are good enough vs. when RANS is required.
