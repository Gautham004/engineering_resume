Custom UAV platforms built end-to-end - frame, electronics, firmware, tuning, and the test flights that prove they actually fly.

Overview

An ongoing personal practice of building and flying small UAVs - both an FPV racing quad and a small autonomous fixed-wing - to keep the path between hardware, firmware, and flight test live.

Approach

  • Spec'd frames, motors, ESCs, and batteries against payload and flight-time targets.
  • Built up the airframes - soldering, harnessing, mounting; pre-flight bench checks before powered tests.
  • Configured PX4 / Betaflight on Pixhawk and SpeedyBee stacks; tuned PIDs over a few flight sessions.
  • Ran flights at sane sites with checklists, and recorded telemetry & video to debrief afterward.

What I Took Away

  • Real-world feel for how firmware tuning translates to flight behavior - not just a sim plot.
  • Hands-on confidence with companion-computer + autopilot stacks that carry directly into research work.
  • Hard-won respect for pre-flight checklists and conservative test envelopes.
