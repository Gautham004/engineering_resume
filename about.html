About

[ portrait · 4:5 ]

I'm an aerospace engineer working at the seam between aerodynamics, autonomous UAV systems, and hands-on flight test - the kind of work where the math has to land on a runway.

I take ideas from a whiteboard to a tested system, pairing first-principles thinking with CAD, simulation, manufacturing, electronics, and code. CFD-driven airfoil studies, ROS2 autonomy stacks, carbon-fiber wings, and the test ops that prove they actually work.

I'm finishing a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering at the University of Oklahoma. Outside class, I lead aero on the AIAA Design/Build/Fly team, contribute to autonomous-flight research at AIROU, and have interned in early-stage aerospace design at Solakair.

Off the field I'm usually building drones, soldering something, or reading propulsion papers I'll only half-understand on the first pass.
